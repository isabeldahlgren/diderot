# Roadmap

Diderot is currently a prototype — a working demonstration with no institution, funding, or editorial staff behind it yet, and running costs presently covered out of pocket. The project is actively seeking institutional support. This page describes, candidly, how it is meant to grow.

## Near-term: content and community

It seems the main challenge is reaching a critical mass of content and the community around it. Two simple things that would help enormously:

- **Joining the [Zulip server](https://diderot.zulipchat.com/join/nkoxw2ud2j5ribx7m4vrlujr/).** Simply being a member counts — there is no need to post.
- **Spreading the word.** If you feel comfortable doing so, share links to the website ([https://projectdiderot.com](https://projectdiderot.com/)) and the [Zulip server](https://diderot.zulipchat.com/join/nkoxw2ud2j5ribx7m4vrlujr/) with researchers, mathematicians, engineers — anyone who might be interested — and ask them to pass it on in turn.

A larger ask: if you have old AI writeups that would not ordinarily go on arXiv, feel free to upload them here. Diderot exists so this work can be citable and archived rather than lost on a company blog.

## Medium-term: structure and funding

If the project gains traction, the plan is to set up a non-profit to deal with the overhead (legal, political, administrative) involved in running a preprint server. The search for institutional support is already underway.

Offers of help are much appreciated.
