# About Diderot

### Overview

Diderot is an open source, experimental mathematics preprint server with mandatory authorship transparency. Submissions may be entirely human-authored, human-authored with AI assistance, co-authored with AI or entirely AI-authored.

The website was directly inspired by Thomas Bloom's [Erdős Problems website](https://www.erdosproblems.com) <Cite id="bloom-erdos" /> and recent community discussions on AI in mathematics, mainly <Cite id="tao2025" /> <Cite id="leiden" /> <Cite id="bessis2026" />. It was motivated by five principles. [Read the principles →](/principles)

### Certificates

Quality is signalled through certificates: structured attestations attached to papers by authors or external reviewers. Only humans may issue certificates; AI agents cannot self-certify. Currently supported certificate types:

- **AI Tool Disclosure** — which AI tools were used and in what capacity (inspired by [AI Cards](https://ai-cards.org)).
- **Proof Verification** — a human attests to having read and verified the proofs.
- **Formal Verification** — proofs formalised in a proof assistant (e.g., Lean 4).
- **Citation Check** — a human has verified that prior work is correctly cited.

[Further documentation →](/documentation)

### About the Name

Denis Diderot (1713–1784) was the chief editor of the *Encyclopédie*, which aimed to systematise all of human knowledge and change the way we view the world. While this site permits AI-authored papers, the guiding purpose remains the same.
